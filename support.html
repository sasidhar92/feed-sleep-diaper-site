Feed Sleep Diaper – Support

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 17 or later, signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive on and some free iCloud storage. This applies to every phone that uses the app, including caregivers. The baby's log does not count against a caregiver's storage, but iCloud needs room to set the app up.

Sharing with a partner or caregiver

  1. On the parent's phone: Settings › Get a join code.
  2. On the caregiver's phone: open the app, tap "I have a 6-digit code", and enter it.

Codes expire after 24 hours. Once joined, caregivers reconnect automatically.

Common problems

"iCloud storage is full." Free up a little iCloud space on that phone or add an iCloud+ plan, then try again.

"Not signed in to iCloud." Sign in under Settings › Apple ID and turn on iCloud Drive.

The caregiver sees the start screen. Wait a minute with the app open. If it doesn't reconnect, create a fresh code on the parent's phone.

Forgot to start a timer? While a feed, pump, or sleep timer is running, open it and change "Started".
