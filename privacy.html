Feed Sleep Diaper – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

The short version

Feed Sleep Diaper does not collect, store, or sell any of your data. We have no servers and no accounts. Everything you log stays in your own iCloud account and on your devices.

What the app stores

The app records what you enter: feeding, pumping, diaper, sleep, and growth entries, your baby's name and birth date, and your unit preferences. This information is saved on your phone and in your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service.

Sharing with caregivers

When you create a join code, the app stores a short-lived link to your shared log in the app's public CloudKit database so a caregiver can connect. The code expires after 24 hours. A caregiver who joins can see and edit the shared log. You can stop sharing at any time by creating a new share from Settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not operate servers or receive your data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties.

Apple's role

Data synced through iCloud is handled under Apple's privacy policy. The app requires an iCloud account with available storage on each phone that uses it.

Children's privacy

The app is used by parents and caregivers to record information about their own children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and no information leaves the family's own iCloud accounts.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app, or delete the app and remove its data under Settings › Apple ID › iCloud › Manage Storage on your phone.
